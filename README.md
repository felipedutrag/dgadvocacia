## 📌 Executive Summary

**DG Advocacia** (headed by **Dr. Felipe Dutra Gonçalves — OAB/SP nº 459.254**) operates as the **technical legal backend and institutional infrastructure** for branding agencies, accounting firms, and intellectual property consultants registering trademarks before the Brazilian Patent and Trademark Office (INPI).

> *"Your company handles client relationships and branding; our legal team powers statutory proceedings, oppositions, appeals, and strategic defense supported by an intelligent real-time partner dashboard."*

---

## 🛠️ Technological Arsenal

1. **💡 Strategic Naming AI:** Generates phonetically distinctive brand names and runs preliminary conflict checks under Article 124 of Brazilian Industrial Property Law (LPI).
2. **🤖 Dra. Sofia (Active Legal AI):** Natural language interface capable of querying INPI proceedings, interpreting official gazettes (RPI), and explaining classification strategies.
3. **📊 Partner Portal & Live Telemetry:** Real-time client pipeline tracking, status alerts, and document repository.
4. **🛡️ OAB Ethical Compliance:** Tailored architecture adhering strictly to professional conduct guidelines and attorney-client privileges.
